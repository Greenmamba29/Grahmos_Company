# GrahmOS Product Roadmap v1

Status: Published draft v1  
Owner: Osiris Hermes (CEO)  
Scope: Company-level roadmap across Hermes, OpenClaw, Accio Source, and JAM Media

## 1. Purpose

This roadmap defines the first coherent operating plan for GrahmOS as an AI
infrastructure company. It aligns the company mission, product sequencing, and
cross-functional execution so brand teams can work from the same strategic
frame.

The roadmap is ordered by phases rather than hard calendar dates. Phase exits
should be gated by evidence, not by schedule pressure.

## 2. North Star

**Become the Apple of AI infrastructure** by delivering a tightly integrated
stack for building, discovering, operating, and commercializing AI agents.

## 3. Strategic principles

1. **Integrated beats fragmented.** GrahmOS products should feel like one
   ecosystem, not a bag of tools.
2. **Agents should produce leverage, not overhead.** Every workflow must reduce
   human toil and increase execution speed.
3. **Trust is a product feature.** Security, observability, provenance, and
   governance must ship alongside capability.
4. **Distribution compounds product quality.** Marketplace, media, and source
   acquisition loops should accelerate one another.
5. **Prefer proof over opinion.** Each phase needs measurable adoption and
   outcome signals before expansion.

## 4. Company objectives

### Objective A: Launch the GrahmOS core control plane

Build the operating layer that helps teams deploy, orchestrate, and manage
agentic systems with confidence.

### Objective B: Establish OpenClaw as the agent distribution surface

Create the default marketplace for trusted, production-ready agents, starting
with high-signal listings and constrained launch workflows.

### Objective C: Turn Accio Source into the data and supplier advantage

Develop sourcing and intelligence capabilities that improve agent performance,
deployment quality, and commercial conversion.

### Objective D: Use JAM Media as the demand engine

Translate product releases, customer stories, and ecosystem activity into
audience growth, pipeline creation, and category authority.

## 5. Phase roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation

### Primary outcome

Define the product surface, operating model, and first publishable assets that
make GrahmOS legible to customers, partners, and internal operators.

### Must-win deliverables

- Publish a company roadmap and shared narrative
- Write the OpenClaw marketplace PRD
- Clarify brand positioning and system boundaries
- Define decision owners across product, engineering, GTM, and content

### Exit signals

- Core strategy docs are published and versioned
- Each brand has a clear role in the ecosystem
- The next implementation issues can be broken into scoped work packages

### Phase 2: Product proof

### Primary outcome

Ship the minimum integrated product loop that proves GrahmOS can create,
discover, and operate useful agents.

### Must-win deliverables

- Hermes control plane alpha for agent management and observability
- OpenClaw marketplace beta with curated listings and deployment intents
- First Accio Source integrations that enrich listings or deployment quality
- JAM Media content engine tied to launches, demos, and thought leadership

### Exit signals

- Repeatable agent submission and approval flow exists
- Users can discover agents and express deployment intent
- Internal teams can measure funnel health across acquisition and usage

### Phase 3: Ecosystem expansion

### Primary outcome

Turn the initial product loop into a defensible platform with stronger trust,
partner participation, and monetization options.

### Must-win deliverables

- Marketplace trust layer: verification, reviews, provenance, policy controls
- Self-serve agent onboarding for qualified builders
- Commercial packaging for paid distribution or managed deployment paths
- Reporting for operator health, category performance, and revenue signals

### Exit signals

- Marketplace supply grows without a drop in trust quality
- GrahmOS can support partner-led launches
- Commercial metrics justify deeper investment

## 6. Brand strategy map

| Brand | Role in the ecosystem | v1 focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hermes | Operating system and control plane for agent infrastructure | Agent lifecycle, management, observability, governance |
| OpenClaw | Discovery and distribution layer | Curated marketplace, listing quality, deployment intent capture |
| Accio Source | Data and sourcing advantage | Inputs that improve agent quality, enrichment, and targeting |
| JAM Media | Narrative and growth engine | Audience building, category education, launch amplification |

## 7. Cross-company KPI framework

### Product KPIs

- Number of launch-ready agents in OpenClaw
- Percentage of listings with verified metadata and operator support
- Deployment intent conversion rate from listing view to next step
- Active operator teams using Hermes workflows

### GTM KPIs

- Qualified marketplace traffic
- Content-sourced leads and partner conversations
- Conversion from ecosystem content to product engagement

### Operating KPIs

- Time to publish a new strategic or product artifact
- Time from strategy approval to delegated execution issue creation
- Percentage of issues closed with a durable artifact or shipped change

## 8. Major dependencies and risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blurry brand boundaries | Confuses customers and slows internal execution | Keep one ecosystem narrative and explicit brand roles |
| Low-trust marketplace supply | A weak catalog harms the brand early | Start curated, review-heavy, and quality-gated |
| Overbuilding before proof | Burns resources without validated demand | Use phase exits and narrow launch surfaces |
| Weak measurement | Makes prioritization subjective | Define funnel and quality metrics before expansion |
