# GrahmOS Hermes Beta Marketing Campaign

## Goal

Launch the Hermes beta with a focused campaign that:

- drives qualified beta signups from technical founders, AI builders, and early-stage infrastructure teams
- positions Hermes as the control layer for reliable AI operations
- creates enough content momentum to support a 30-day launch window without ad spend dependence

## Campaign Summary

- **Campaign name:** Build Faster. Operate Smarter.
- **Primary offer:** Apply for the GrahmOS Hermes beta
- **Primary CTA:** Join the beta waitlist
- **Secondary CTA:** Book a founder discovery call
- **Launch window:** 30 days
- **North-star outcome:** consistent demand generation for Hermes as the flagship GrahmOS product

## Target Audience

### Primary audience

1. AI startup founders building agentic products
2. technical operators managing LLM workflows in production
3. engineering leaders evaluating AI infrastructure tooling

### Secondary audience

1. solo developers shipping AI apps quickly
2. consultants and agencies implementing AI systems for clients
3. technical creators covering AI infrastructure, agents, and automation

## Core Positioning

Hermes helps teams move from prototype AI workflows to dependable operating systems for production-grade AI execution.

### Positioning statement

**For AI teams that need reliable execution, Hermes is the AI operations layer that turns scattered tools, prompts, and workflows into a controlled system that can ship, monitor, and improve over time.**

## Messaging Pillars

### 1. From demos to dependable systems

Most AI products work in a demo and break in production. Hermes gives teams structure, repeatability, and operational clarity.

### 2. AI infrastructure without platform sprawl

Hermes reduces the need to stitch together too many dashboards, scripts, and brittle automations.

### 3. Operate agents with confidence

Teams need visibility, accountability, and control for agent-driven work. Hermes makes that operational layer legible.

### 4. Built for speed without sacrificing governance

Hermes is for teams that want fast shipping, clear ownership, and disciplined execution.

## Narrative Angles

- why AI teams need an operating system, not just more model wrappers
- the hidden cost of unmanaged agents and ad hoc automation
- how Hermes makes AI execution feel more like product infrastructure than experimentation
- why founders should fix operational reliability before scaling model usage

## Offer and Conversion Path

### Top-of-funnel

- founder-led social posts on X and LinkedIn
- short-form educational content
- launch narrative threads
- teaser product visuals and workflow screenshots

### Middle-of-funnel

- landing page with clear beta value proposition
- founder note explaining who Hermes is for
- waitlist form with role, company stage, and use-case capture
- one concise explainer deck or page

### Bottom-of-funnel

- beta acceptance emails
- founder calls for qualified prospects
- onboarding sequence for accepted beta users

## Channel Plan

### Owned channels

- X
- LinkedIn
- company landing page
- email waitlist follow-up

### Earned / relationship channels

- founder and operator communities
- partner resharing from personal accounts
- targeted outreach to technical creators

## Content System

The content plan should repeat four patterns:

1. **Problem posts** - highlight pain around unreliable AI operations
2. **Point-of-view posts** - define the Hermes thesis
3. **Proof posts** - demos, screenshots, workflows, and product logic
4. **Conversion posts** - direct calls to join the beta

## Launch Phases

### Phase 1: Warm-up

- establish the problem space
- publish founder POV content
- seed the language around AI operations and governance

### Phase 2: Reveal

- announce Hermes beta
- release core visuals and landing page messaging
- start direct beta CTA cadence

### Phase 3: Proof

- publish product walkthroughs
- share use cases, operating principles, and behind-the-scenes build notes
- answer objections in public

### Phase 4: Conversion

- create urgency around beta slots
- post testimonials, early interest, and recap content
- push final call-to-action posts

## Success Metrics

### Primary metrics

- beta waitlist signups
- qualified beta applicants
- founder calls booked

### Secondary metrics

- landing page conversion rate
- X engagement rate
- LinkedIn engagement rate
- email reply rate from accepted applicants

- keep the tone founder-led, technical, and opinionated
- avoid generic AI marketing language
- anchor every week around one clear narrative theme
- reuse high-performing posts into threads, carousels, and short videos
- collect signups with enough qualification data to prioritize onboarding
