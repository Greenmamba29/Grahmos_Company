## Weekly Themes

### Week 1: Frame the problem

Define the pain around unreliable AI operations and establish the Hermes worldview.

### Week 2: Reveal Hermes

Announce the beta, explain the product clearly, and make the waitlist CTA visible.

### Week 3: Prove the thesis

Show product logic, use cases, and concrete benefits for technical operators.

### Week 4: Convert demand

Increase urgency, answer objections, and channel attention into waitlist applications and calls.

## Reuse Plan

- Turn Days 1, 3, 19, and 24 into email copy and landing page snippets.
- Convert Days 8, 9, 18, and 26 into a lightweight media kit for partner amplification.
- Package Days 6, 17, 23, and 29 into an FAQ page or pinned thread.

## Measurement Cadence

- Review engagement daily.
- Review signup quality twice per week.
- Double down on formats with strong saves, replies, and click-throughs.
- Rewrite underperforming conversion posts instead of simply repeating them.
